FOA Sound Separation – Audio Examples

Each example contains: Mixture, GT and Prediction for two sources, plus a small waveform overview and a unit-circle direction plot to indicate DOA to the listener.
Headphones recommended. Audio levels may vary — start at a low volume.
Model outputs are FOA, rendered binaurally for listening.
FOA (ACN/SN3D) → N3D → magLS binaural decoder (order 1) using FABIAN HATO HRIRs (SOFA), rendered at 16 kHz.
